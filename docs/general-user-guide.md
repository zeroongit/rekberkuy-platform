# RekberKuy — General User Guide

A friendly, task-based guide for everyone using RekberKuy: buyers, sellers, service providers, event organizers, and vendors. Pick a goal below and follow the steps. No technical knowledge needed.

> RekberKuy is an escrow platform that locks your payment safely until the goods, service, or event is delivered — then releases the money to the right person. Every completed transaction is also recorded as a tamper-proof receipt on the Avalanche blockchain.

## 1. Getting Started

### How to create an account

Anyone can join RekberKuy for free as a basic user.

1. Open the **Register** page.
2. Fill in your **name, email, phone number, and a password**.
3. We send a **verification email** to the address you entered.
4. Open the email and **click the verification link**.
5. Your account is activated and you land on your dashboard.

**Good to know**
- If the email is already registered, we'll let you know and point you to the login page.
- If the verification link has expired, you can request a new one from the same screen.
- A basic account lets you buy goods and tickets. To **sell, run events, or become a vendor**, you also need to verify your identity (see below).

---

### How to log in (and what to do if you get locked out)

1. Enter your **email and password**.
2. We check your credentials and sign you in to your dashboard.

**Good to know**
- After **3 wrong password attempts**, your account is temporarily locked for **15 minutes** for security. Just wait and try again.
- Forgot your password? Use the **Forgot password** link and we'll email you a reset link.

---

### How to verify your identity (KYC)

**Who needs KYC:** Sellers, Service Providers, Event Organizers, and Vendors. Everyday buyers who only purchase do **not** need KYC, but sellers/organizers must be verified before they can receive payouts.

**Why:** Identity verification protects everyone on the platform from fraud and is required before funds can be released to you.

1. Open the **KYC** menu from your dashboard.
2. Upload clear photos of your **ID card (KTP/SIM)** — front and back.
3. Upload a **selfie photo holding your ID card**.
4. Fill in your **personal data exactly as it appears on the ID card**.
5. Submit. Our team reviews your documents within **24 hours**.
6. You'll get a notification when verification is complete, and a **"Verified" badge** appears on your profile.

**Good to know**
- If documents are blurry or the data doesn't match the ID, the review will be **rejected** and you'll be asked to re-upload.
- If you believe a rejection was a mistake, you can file an appeal.

## 2. Your Wallet (RekberPay)

Every user has a **RekberPay wallet**. Money in your wallet is what funds transactions, and it's also where your incoming payments land.

### How to top up your wallet

1. Open the **Wallet** menu.
2. Choose the **top-up amount**.
3. Pick a **payment method** (bank transfer or e-wallet).
4. We create an invoice through our payment partner **Midtrans**.
5. Complete the payment in the method you chose.
6. Your wallet balance is updated and you get a confirmation notification.

**Good to know**
- Top-ups are processed automatically once Midtrans confirms your payment. If your balance doesn't update after a successful payment, wait a few minutes — the bank callback can take a short moment.

---

### How to withdraw money to your bank account

Sellers, service providers, event organizers, and vendors can withdraw earnings to a real bank account.

1. Go to **Wallet → Withdraw Funds**.
2. Enter the **amount** and choose your **destination bank account**.
3. We check that your balance covers the withdrawal **plus the Rp 7.500 withdrawal fee**.
4. Confirm. We deduct the balance and queue the transfer.
5. The money arrives in your bank account within **1 business day (T+1)**.
6. You'll be notified when the transfer is complete.

**Good to know**
- The withdrawal fee is a **flat Rp 7.500** per withdrawal, deducted together with the amount you withdraw. You must have enough balance to cover both.
- Withdrawals are processed on business days, so transfers started on weekends or holidays arrive on the next business day.

## 3. Buying & Selling Goods

### How to create a goods transaction (as a buyer)

1. Select **Create Goods Transaction**.
2. Fill in the details:
   - **Goods name & description**
   - **Goods price**
   - **Seller** (their username or email)
   - **Shipping method**
3. We calculate the **total** = price + shipping + platform fee.
4. Confirm the transaction. The total is **deducted from your wallet and locked safely in escrow**.
5. The seller is notified of a new order.
6. The transaction is recorded as a permanent audit entry.

**Good to know**
- If your wallet balance is too low, you'll be redirected to **top up** first.
- The seller must already have a RekberKuy account. If the username/email isn't found, you'll see an error.
- Your money is **not** sent to the seller yet — it's held in escrow until you confirm receipt (or the auto-confirm rule kicks in).

---

### How to ship an order (as a seller)

1. You'll get a notification of a new incoming transaction.
2. Open the transaction and **confirm you're willing to ship**.
3. Once you've dispatched the goods, **enter the shipping tracking number**.
4. The order status updates to **"In Shipping"**.
5. The buyer is notified that the goods are on the way.

**Good to know**
- Enter the tracking number as soon as you have it — the buyer's confirmation window and the auto-confirm timer are based on shipping.

---

### How to confirm you received the goods (as a buyer)

1. Once the goods arrive, open the transaction.
2. Tap **"Goods Received"**.
3. The escrow funds are **released to the seller's wallet**.
4. The seller is notified of incoming funds.
5. The transaction is marked **"Completed"** and the audit log is finalized.

**Good to know**
- Only confirm after you've actually received and checked the item. If something is wrong, do **not** confirm — instead, [report a problem](#how-to-report-a-problem-with-a-transaction).

---

### The 3-day auto-confirm rule

If you **do not confirm receipt within 3 days of the estimated arrival date**, the system assumes the order was delivered successfully and **automatically releases the funds to the seller**. The transaction is then marked Completed.

This protects sellers from orders that are received but never confirmed. If there's a genuine problem, [report it before the window closes](#how-to-report-a-problem-with-a-transaction) — reporting a valid problem freezes the funds.

## 4. Services & Milestone Work

### How to create a service contract

Use this when you're hiring (or being hired for) freelance or professional work that's best paid in phases.

1. Select **Create Services Transaction** (as the client).
2. Fill in the details:
   - **Job name & description**
   - **Service provider** (username/email)
   - **Total contract value**
   - **Milestone list** — for each milestone add a name, an amount, and a deadline
3. The service provider **confirms they agree** with the terms.
4. You deposit the **total contract value into escrow** up front (it's locked, not paid out yet).
5. The contract goes **active** and the first milestone begins.

**Good to know**
- Because the full value is locked in escrow, the service provider knows the funds are guaranteed, and you know the money only releases as milestones are approved.

---

### How milestone review and payment works

1. The service provider marks a milestone as **completed** and uploads the **work result / proof of completion**.
2. You receive a notification to **review the work**.
3. Open the milestone, review the deliverable, then either:
   - **Approve** it, or
   - **Request a revision** (see below).
4. When you approve, the **milestone funds are released to the service provider's wallet**, and the next milestone begins.

**Good to know (auto-release):** If you **don't respond within 3 days** of a milestone being submitted, the milestone funds are **automatically released** to the service provider. Review submissions promptly so you keep control.

---

### How to request a revision

1. While reviewing a submitted milestone, choose **Request Revision**.
2. Explain what needs changing.
3. The service provider updates the work and **re-uploads** the result.
4. You review again — repeat until you're happy, then approve.

The auto-release timer resets each time a new version is submitted for your review.

## 5. Events (for Organizers & Participants)

### How to create an event (as an Event Organizer)

> You must complete **KYC verification** before you can create events.

1. Select **Create New Event**.
2. Fill in the event details:
   - **Name, description, date, and location**
   - **Category** (concert, seminar, party, etc.)
   - **Capacity** and **ticket price**
3. Set up your vendor needs:
   - Choose a vendor from the **RekberKuy marketplace**, **or**
   - Register an **external vendor** (name, contact, contract value).
4. Submit the event for Admin review (optional).
5. Once published, your event is ready to accept participants.

---

### How to buy a ticket (as a participant)

1. **Browse or search** for the event you want to attend.
2. Choose the **quantity and ticket category**.
3. We calculate the **total + platform fee**.
4. Pay using your **wallet balance** or another payment method through Midtrans.
5. We generate a **digital ticket with a QR code**.
6. Your ticket arrives **by email and inside the app**.
7. Your payment goes into the event's escrow.

**Good to know**
- Show the QR code at the event entrance. If the event is cancelled, your money is automatically refunded to your wallet (see below).

---

### How to pay a vendor for your event

1. From the event page, create a **vendor contract**.
2. Define the vendor's **payment milestones**.
3. Deposit the **vendor funds into escrow**.
4. The vendor completes the work against the milestones.
5. You **confirm the vendor's work** for each milestone.
6. The funds are **released to the vendor's wallet**.
7. The vendor payment is recorded on the audit trail.

---

### How event settlement works (surplus, refunds, and cancellation)

**When the event is completed successfully:**

1. You mark the event as **Completed**.
2. We add up all vendor costs and the total ticket revenue.
3. The system distributes the money automatically:
   - **Platform fee** is sent to RekberKuy.
   - Each **partner vendor** receives their net allocation.
   - **You (the EO)** receive your management fee **plus any surplus** — surplus is the money left over when ticket sales exceed your vendor costs. Think of it as the profit from running a successful event.

**If the event is cancelled:**

1. You cancel the event.
2. **All participants are automatically refunded** to their wallets.
3. Vendor contracts are terminated and any vendor funds are **returned to you**.

**Good to know**
- "Surplus" simply means your event earned more than it cost. The bigger and better-attended your event, the more you may earn on top of your management fee.

## 6. Vendor Marketplace

The Vendor Marketplace is where Event Organizers find trusted providers for things like **catering, decoration, photo/video, and sound systems**.

### How to register as a vendor

1. Open **Register as a Vendor** (you need a RekberKuy account first).
2. Build your business profile:
   - **Business name & service description**
   - **Category** (catering, decoration, photo/video, sound system, etc.)
   - **Service area** (city / province)
   - **Portfolio** (photos & documents)
   - **Starting price**
3. Upload your **legal documents** (SIUP, NPWP, etc.).
4. Submit. Our team reviews your registration within **48 hours**.
5. Once approved, you appear in the marketplace with a **"Verified Vendor" badge**.

**Good to know**
- The Verified Vendor badge tells organizers your business is legitimate, which builds trust and helps you win contracts.

---

### How an organizer finds and contracts you

1. An Event Organizer opens the **Find Vendor** tab on their event page.
2. They **filter by category, city, and budget**.
3. They view your **profile and portfolio**.
4. They **contact you via the platform chat** to agree on details and pricing.
5. Once you both agree, the EO creates a **vendor contract** — the funds go into escrow.
6. You're notified of the new contract and can begin work.

**Good to know**
- Because the EO deposits the funds into escrow up front, you're guaranteed payment when you complete the work. Funds land in your wallet, and you can withdraw to your bank (see [How to withdraw](#how-to-withdraw-money-to-your-bank-account)).

## 7. Disputes & Protection

### How to report a problem with a transaction

If something is wrong with an order — wrong item, damaged goods, item never arrived — or if you want to leave a rating below 3 stars:

1. Open the transaction page and select **Report a Problem / Give a Low Rating**.
2. Because this is a serious claim, you'll be required to **upload an unboxing video** (see the rule below).
3. Upload an **uncut unboxing video** as evidence.
4. Our automated system reviews the video for tampering and reads the tracking number from it.
5. If the claim is judged **valid**:
   - The funds in escrow are **frozen**.
   - The seller is notified and an Admin steps in to mediate.
6. The Admin reviews everything and makes a **final decision**.
7. The decision is carried out and recorded on the audit trail.

**Good to know**
- If you **don't upload a video**, or the video shows signs of editing/cuts, the report is **automatically rejected** before it's even recorded. Make sure your video is a single continuous take from opening the package onward.

---

### The unboxing video rule

For any low rating (under 3 stars) or goods defect claim, an **uncut unboxing video is mandatory**. The video must be one continuous recording that clearly shows:

- The sealed package,
- You opening it, and
- The contents (including the tracking number visible somewhere, e.g., on the label).

This protects honest buyers and sellers alike — it's the single best piece of evidence if something goes wrong.

---

### How mediation works

1. When a valid dispute freezes the funds, an **Admin mediator** is assigned.
2. The mediator reviews the AI's findings, the unboxing video, and any messages between buyer and seller.
3. The mediator issues a **final resolution** (for example: full refund, partial refund, or release to seller).
4. The platform executes the decision and the result is permanently logged.

**Good to know**
- Mediation decisions are final and tamper-proof — they're written to the same audit trail as the original transaction.

---

### What is the blockchain audit trail?

Every completed transaction on RekberKuy is recorded on the **Avalanche blockchain** — think of it as a **tamper-proof digital receipt**.

- It records the transaction's ID, its timestamp, and its final status.
- Once written, **no one — not even RekberKuy — can alter or delete it**.
- It lets anyone independently verify that a transaction happened exactly as stated.

**Good to know**
- You don't need a crypto wallet or any technical setup. RekberKuy handles the blockchain recording for you automatically and covers the cost (a "gasless" experience). You just see the verifiable proof on your transaction page.

## 8. Roles & Verification Badges

| Role / Badge | What it means | How to unlock it |
|--------------|---------------|------------------|
| **User** | A registered account holder. Can buy goods and tickets, and top up a wallet. | Create an account and verify your email. |
| **Verified Merchant** | A seller or service provider who has passed identity verification. Can receive escrow payouts and withdraw to a bank. | Complete [KYC](#how-to-verify-your-identity-kyc). |
| **Verified Vendor** | A business listed in the Vendor Marketplace that has passed business-document review. | [Register as a vendor](#how-to-register-as-a-vendor) and pass Admin review. |
| **Event Organizer (EO)** | A verified user who can create events, manage vendors, and receive event settlement payouts. | Complete KYC, then create an event. |
| **Admin** | A member of the RekberKuy team. Handles KYC/vendor approvals, mediates disputes, and keeps the platform safe. | Internal RekberKuy staff only. |

**Good to know**
- Verification badges build trust. The more verified you are, the more likely other users are to transact with you confidently.

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions

**Is my money safe while a transaction is in progress?**
Yes. Your payment is locked in escrow and is only released when the agreed conditions are met — either you confirm receipt, the milestone is approved, or a mediator decides the outcome.

**Do I need a crypto wallet to use RekberKuy?**
No. The blockchain is used only as a tamper-proof receipt. RekberKuy handles everything behind the scenes — you never touch crypto.

**What if I receive a damaged item?**
Do **not** confirm receipt. Instead, [report a problem](#how-to-report-a-problem-with-a-transaction) and upload an unboxing video. Your funds stay protected while the dispute is reviewed.

**Can I cancel an event after tickets have been sold?**
Yes. If you cancel, all participants are automatically refunded to their wallets, and any vendor funds are returned to you.

**Who needs to complete KYC?**
Sellers, service providers, event organizers, and vendors. Everyday buyers who only purchase goods or tickets do not need KYC.

**How long do withdrawals take?**
Up to 1 business day (T+1), with a flat Rp 7.500 fee per withdrawal.
